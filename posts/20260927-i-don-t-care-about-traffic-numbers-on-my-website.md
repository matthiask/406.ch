Title: I don't care about traffic numbers on my website
Categories: Django, Programming
Draft: remove-this-to-publish

In the early days of this website I had an Google Analytics script embedded and
I also sometimes analyzed the server logs to look at visitor counts and where
they came from.

While that was interesting I have completely stopped doing that.

I do care about human readers and I also do care whether people are
appreciative of what I'm doing. There's no way to programmatically measure that
though so I'm not even going to try.

These days, the primary audience of my writing is myself, since writing helps
formulate thoughts -- when thinking about stuff it's easy to delude yourself
into thinking that you have a clear story and a coherent argument. The writing
reveals whether that's complete bullshit or if it might be true. Writing still
isn't the ultimate test of an idea of course, otherwise we'd have solved
philosophy a long time ago.

Back to the initial idea of the blog post. Caring about traffic numbers makes
me care about the wrong thing. I like the calm internet of the blogging days.
They might happen again when people have enough of algorithmic social media
outrage.
